All calculators include

💧Fluid balance guidance with IWL from published literature (Ågren 2022, Dereddy 2025)
🎯Target intake range for 2–3%/day weight loss, floored at PN+Lipid nutritional minimum
⚠️Safety warnings for high glucose concentration, lipid volume, urine output, and PN floor
📅Day-of-life aware: PN advancement floors (Day 1–2: 71, Day 3+: 81 ml/kg/day)
🥛Enteral feeds including maternal & donor breast milk (fortified), Nutriprem 1 & 2
💾Auto-saves your last regimen locally — data stays on your device
Clinical Decision Support Only. These calculators are intended to assist clinicians and do not replace clinical judgement. All fluid prescriptions must be reviewed and authorised by a qualified clinician. Values calculated here should be verified before administration.
